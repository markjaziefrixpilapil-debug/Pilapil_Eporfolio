Prelim Getting Started: My Initial Impressions

The preliminary period marked the beginning of what turned out to be a transformative academic experience. When classes first started, I was filled with a mixture of anticipation and uncertainty. The workload felt overwhelming at first, and the new concepts introduced were challenging to absorb all at once. However, as the weeks progressed, I began to find my footing and develop a rhythm that worked for me.

Prelim Lessons Learned and Early Challenges

One of the most significant lessons I took away from the preliminary period was the importance of time management. Balancing coursework, requirements, and personal responsibilities taught me that discipline is not something that comes naturally — it is something you build one decision at a time. I learned to prioritize tasks, break them into manageable steps, and approach deadlines with a proactive mindset rather than waiting until the last moment.

Prelim Growth Through Uncertainty

Looking back on the preliminary term, I now recognize that the discomfort I felt early on was not a setback but a signal of growth. Every topic that confused me became an opportunity to ask better questions, seek support from classmates, and deepen my understanding. By the end of this period, I had not only gained academic knowledge but also a stronger sense of self-confidence and a clearer picture of the student I aspired to become.
