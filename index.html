Term Reflections

These reflections focus on the learning experiences in each term of Computer Programming 1.

01

Prelim Term Reflection

In the Prelim Term, I learned the foundations of Java through branching, arrays, loops, and decision-making activities. Some tasks were difficult at first, but they helped me understand how careful logic and correct syntax affect the result of a program.

02

Midterm Reflection

In the Midterm Term, I practiced functions, methods, parameters, return values, arrays, and exceptions. These activities taught me how to organize code into smaller parts and review mistakes more carefully when my output was not correct.

03

Final Term Reflection

In the Final Term, I worked on harder topics such as stacks, queues, sorting, and case study coding. These tasks challenged me to think faster and apply previous lessons, especially in solving problems step by step under time limits.
